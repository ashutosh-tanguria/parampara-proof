# Code of conduct

We want this to be a respectful place to learn about and build around traditional Indian art.

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Treat the art forms and the communities they come from with respect. Do not mock, devalue or misrepresent them.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Be honest about what the software can and cannot do. Do not make claims of authenticity, expert endorsement or artist involvement that are not true.
- Credit other people's work and follow image licences.

Maintainers may remove comments, code or contributions that break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers listed in the README.

This is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org>).
